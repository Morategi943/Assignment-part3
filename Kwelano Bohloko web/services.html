Our Services

Services and activities designed to support our community.

01

Community Support

We provide support to older persons and people living with disabilities within the community.

02

Social Activities

Community activities can help members remain active, connected and involved with others.

03

Information and Assistance

We help community members find information and connect with appropriate support where possible.

04

Community Partnerships

We welcome partnerships with businesses, volunteers, organisations and community groups.

Volunteer Opportunities

Volunteers can contribute their time, skills and knowledge to support the centre.

Support for Families

Families can contact us to learn more about available support and community resources.

Find the Support That Fits You

Older persons and their families can ask about community support, companionship and social activities. Use our inquiry form to tell us what you need.

People living with disabilities and their carers are welcome to ask about inclusion, information and assistance. Our team will explain what is available. Contact the centre to start the conversation.

Volunteers and donors can give time, skills, food, supplies or equipment. See the items the centre needs, or make an inquiry to offer your help.
